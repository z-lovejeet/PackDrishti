28. Registration of shorter address permissible.-(1) It shall be lawful for any
manufacturer or packer to make an application to the Director or the Controller for the
registration of a shorter address, in addition to the complete address referred to in sub-rule
(2) of rule 27.

(2) The Director or the Controller may, if he is satisfied after inquiry that the shorter address
is sufficient to enable the consumer or any other person to identify the manufacturer or the
packer, register such shorter address.
